About LOCO

Connect. Discover. Thrive Locally.

LOCO is a local community marketplace and skill-sharing platform built to help neighbours buy, sell, hire and learn from one another. Whether you're clearing out old furniture, looking for a reliable tutor, or hoping to trade a skill you know for one you'd like to learn, LOCO brings it all into a single, trusted community space.
